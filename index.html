Frequently Asked Questions

(Everything you need to know about FundRaja)

Is FundRaja really free?
Yes, creating a campaign is free. We do not charge a commission on the funds you raise.
How do I get the money?
It's instant! Since donors pay directly to your UPI ID (GooglePay, PhonePe, Paytm, etc.), the money goes straight to your bank account immediately.
Is it safe?
Absolutely. We vet every campaign before it goes live to ensure authenticity. Payments happen via secure UPI apps you already trust.
What documents are needed?
To ensure trust and safety, we require basic KYC verification (Aadhaar/PAN) before your campaign goes live to the public.
Can I raise funds for personal reasons?
Yes, you can raise funds for medical emergencies, education, creative projects, or personal needs, provided they comply with our community guidelines. FundRaja is also used by NGOs for social causes.
